# Patch

The grove window's trees look more like lemon trees: a domed crown wider than it is tall over a flat, slightly uneven underside, with leafy light from the upper left instead of round clumps, an outline, a shadow and a short forked trunk, and no faint seams between their pixels. Lemons are small pixel lemons of a few shapes instead of circles, and each tree hangs its own way, its lemons apart and never side by side like a pair of eyes.

The grove window's stream is drawn in the same pixel style: deep water and shallows streaked along the flow, muddy banks and grass, with reeds, stones and lily pads along its edges.

The grove window draws stumps where trees whose sessions ended stood: bare pixel trees, a recent one keeping a few dead leaves and its tree's name, as in the terminal grove. `grove show --json` now lists each block's stumps.
