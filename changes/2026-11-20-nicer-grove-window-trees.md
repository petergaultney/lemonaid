# Patch

The grove window's trees look more like lemon trees: a domed crown wider than it is tall over a flat, slightly uneven underside, with leafy light from the upper left instead of round clumps, an outline, a shadow and a short forked trunk, and no faint seams between their pixels. Lemons are small pixel lemons of a few shapes instead of circles, and each tree hangs its own way, its lemons apart and never side by side like a pair of eyes.

The grove window's stream is drawn in the same pixel style, as quiet as the ground: dark water with a few faint streaks along the flow, muddy banks, a little grass, and now and then a reed, stone or lily pad.

The grove window draws stumps where trees whose sessions ended stood: bare pixel trees, a recent one keeping a few dead leaves and its tree's name, as in the terminal grove. `grove show --json` now lists each block's stumps.

Roads, shadows, the packhouse's roof, frames and lemons, list borders, and the dashed frames of a drag are drawn in the same pixel style, and lead trees' flags are pixel pennants.

In the grove window, a bare tree can be gardened as in the terminal grove: dragged within its block to move it, or off its block's ground to clear it.

The grove window's block ground is drawn in the same pixel style: plain soil in the block's color, faintly varied, with tufts of grass in a few loose patches. The ground between the blocks has a fainter, colorless version of it that keeps a translucent window translucent.
