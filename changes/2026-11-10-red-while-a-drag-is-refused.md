# Patch

#### Changed

- **A grove drag turns red while it can't be dropped where it is.** A tree, bare tree, block or open ground is tinted red there, and the bottom line's reason turns red with it, so you see a refused spot before you let go.
- **Backspace with nothing typed closes the grove, as `esc` does.** After a Backspace that cleared something, it waits for a 0.6-second pause between presses, so a held or double-tapped Backspace stops at the whole grove. The inbox's grove view does the same.
