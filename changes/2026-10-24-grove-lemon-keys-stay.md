# Minor

- **A lemon keeps its key as others join its tree.** A tree's first lemon is always its letter, as `5s`, and HQ always `0l`. The rest are the capital and their place on the tree, as `5S2`; past nine, `0` and a letter, as `5S0a`. Before, a third lemon renumbered every lemon on the tree, and on a tree of ten `1` waited in case `10` followed.
