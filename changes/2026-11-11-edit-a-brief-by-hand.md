- **`e` in `lma`, and `lemonaid brief edit <lemon>`, edit a lemon's brief in your editor, from the inbox or from a brief view.** You edit a copy; when you quit, lemonaid merges your change with what the lemon wrote to the brief meanwhile, shows conflicting lines between markers for you to resolve, and tells the lemon what changed so its next write starts from your version.
