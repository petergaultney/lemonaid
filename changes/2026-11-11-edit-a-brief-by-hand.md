# Minor

#### Added

- **`e` in `lma`, and `lemonaid brief edit <lemon>`, edit a lemon's brief in your editor without losing what the lemon writes meanwhile.** You edit a copy; when you quit, lemonaid merges your change into the brief as it is by then, shows conflicting lines between markers for you to resolve, and tells the lemon what changed so its next write starts from your version.
