# Minor

- **The grove has a select mode, where keys choose a lemon without jumping to it and a card shows it.** `Tab` enters it and the screen's edges turn yellow; `5s` chooses a lemon, `5.s1` another on its tree and `5$s` the whole tree, and the chosen lemon's inbox card opens beside its tree's list. With a choice made, `r`, `s`, `a`, `p`, `g` and `c` rename, snooze, archive, pin, move or recolor it, `↑` and `↓` step along the tree, arrows move a whole tree, `enter` or `o` jumps, and `esc` leaves; `grove_select_key` picks another key.
- **Select mode replaces the grove's Alt edits.** `Alt`+`r`, `s`, `a`, `p`, `g` and `c` no longer edit; choose the lemon or tree with `Tab` and press the letter alone. Recolor a block with `c` on any of its trees.
