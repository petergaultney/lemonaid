# Minor

#### Added

- **The web grove marks lemons read and unread by hand.** Holding a lemon offers "Mark unread" or "Mark read", and a lemon marked unread stays unread until you read it again or it reports. A lemon marked unread by hand sends no push notification.
- **Unread shows on a group's row, and on the desktop grove.** The phone's group list counts a group's unread lemons and sorts by them; the desktop grove marks unread lemons in yellow and counts them in its summary.
