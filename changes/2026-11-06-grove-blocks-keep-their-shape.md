# Minor

#### Changed

- **Grove blocks keep their size and shape, clear of the roads and the stream.** A block no longer grows when it gains trees; a new tree in a full block is drawn smaller and squeezed in, nearer trees in front. New blocks are sized for their trees and one more, and are laid out off the roads and the stream. A saved block over them is trimmed off them, or moved whole when trimming would take half its ground, and moving or resizing one onto them is refused.
- **A grove tree in a roomy block is drawn no larger than grown.**
