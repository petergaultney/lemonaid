# Minor

#### Added

- **Choose a lemon independently of its session shape.** Configure named lemon presets with default arguments and separate window layouts, then combine either at launch with extra harness arguments after `--`. Existing templates and `--harness` names keep working.
- **Inspect a launch before starting it.** `lemon presets` lists named lemons, `lemon presets show` resolves their commands and shapes, and `place open` and `lemon start` accept `--dry-run`.
