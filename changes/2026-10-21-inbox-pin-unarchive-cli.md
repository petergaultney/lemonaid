# Minor

#### Added

- **`lemonaid inbox pin`, `inbox unpin` and `inbox unarchive` do from the command line what `P` and the archive undo do in `lma`.** `pin --move up|down|top|bottom` orders pins; archiving still drops a pin, and unarchive does not restore it.
- **`lemonaid brief attach` onto a different channel carries the old channel's pin to the new one.**
