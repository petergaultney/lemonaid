# Minor

#### Added

- **A phone can edit a lemon.** A lemon's page has an `edit` button in place of `pin`, and holding a lemon in a group's list or the bar opens the same sheet: rename, snooze, pin or unpin, move to another group, or archive, made by the grove window's own edits. What an edit did, or why it didn't, stays on screen until you close it.
- **A lemon's page clears a connection problem once it passes.** A refresh nothing answered says "Can't reach the grove; trying again" in place of the browser's "Failed to fetch", and the next refresh that succeeds clears it. The page also notes in the web grove's log how long it took to open.
- **A phone draws a lemon's conversation at once.** It keeps the conversations of the bar's lemons and the lemon last opened, read ahead by the list of groups each time one reports, and a lemon's page draws its kept copy while it asks for what changed. Nothing is kept open for a lemon whose page isn't shown.
