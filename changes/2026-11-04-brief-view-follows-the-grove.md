# Minor

#### Added

- **The sidebar brief follows you between lemons.** A brief open in the scratch sidebar stays open when you switch windows or sessions, from the grove or anywhere else, and shows the lemon you land on. A window with no lemon, such as a shell, keeps the brief you were reading. The session-name bar over a session's briefs is no longer yellow, which is kept for statuses.
- **The brief view's top line says what needs you anywhere.** It is attention mode's status summary from the inbox, its badges in the same columns, and for 10 seconds after a lemon's brief enters a status that status's badge pulses (`[tui] status_pulse_seconds`).
