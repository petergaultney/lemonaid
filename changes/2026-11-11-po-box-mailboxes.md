# Minor

#### Added

- **PO boxes.** A lemon whose brief says `po-box: true` (`lemonaid brief po-box`) gets a second mailbox in a shared folder set as `[po-box] root`, such as an Obsidian Relay shared folder, and teammates' lemons send to it with `lemonaid tell <owner>/<lemon>`. The lemon's watcher opens, renews and closes the box, and its mail arrives with the local mail under `[po-box]`, labeled as unverified and not from the lemon's user. See `docs/po-box.md`.
