# Minor

#### Added

- **Brief help and saved-answer keys.** `?` in the brief view shows that view's keys in the same styled panel as the inbox help. `A` archives the selected saved answer, `Ctrl+A` archives all live answers in its brief, and `e` expands or collapses archived answers without restoring them to the brief.

- **Select Needs and answers by keyboard or mouse.** A colored border and action hint identify the selected entry. Click to select, then click the selected entry again to answer a Need, archive a saved answer, or collapse an archived answer; links and text-selection gestures keep their usual behavior.

#### Fixed

- Keep entry selection safe while a brief is opening or redrawing, avoiding an inbox crash when its widgets are still mounting.
