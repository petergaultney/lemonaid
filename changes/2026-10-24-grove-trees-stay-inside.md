# Patch

- **Trees stay inside their block.** A tree with no room left took ground past its block's edge, sometimes on another block. Now it takes its smallest size first, then the block grows on a side that gives it room, over open ground if need be, and if the block still can't grow the tree squeezes in among the others. A tree's crown may rise above the top of its block. A tree already standing outside moves back in once.
